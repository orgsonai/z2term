1.0.0 — stable

- The first stable release.
- You can now connect to OpenSSH sshd.
- Switching from root to another user really appears as that user,
  and su / sudo take you back to root from there.
- Long-press the tab "+" to pick an OS and open just that tab in it.
- Android commands (cmd / pm / am) return their output in a tab.
- Action macros no longer stop after a tap.
- Files without an execute bit cannot be run directly, as on Linux
  (files on shared storage still can).
