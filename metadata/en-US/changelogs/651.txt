0.8.643-alpha

- First release on F-Droid.
- Place terminal, GUI, VNC and RDP tabs side by side or on an external
  display: long-press the 🖥 button next to +.
- Edge panels gain generic views and forms. `z2-view --edge` sends HTML;
  entered values go to a macro.
- `z2-file pick/save` opens and exports via Android's document picker.
- Process text selected in other apps with a snippet and return it.
- Rooted chroot engine: installing packages no longer fails despite free
  space.
