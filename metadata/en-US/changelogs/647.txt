0.8.639-alpha

- First release on F-Droid.
- On the hidden "chroot" engine for rooted devices, installing a package
  failed with `not enough free disk space` even with plenty free. The rootfs
  is now bind-mounted onto itself so it appears as `/`.
- Added since 0.8.483: edge panels that float over other apps, recording and
  replaying actions, QR tools, `z2-shot` to capture part of the screen,
  `z2-view` to read a page built on the terminal, and a rebuilt key layout
  editor.
