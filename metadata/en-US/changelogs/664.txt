0.9.1 — release candidate

- First release on F-Droid.
- Split view: drag a title to swap panes, double-tap for one pane.
- A narrowed keyboard floats and can be moved freely.
- Edge panels are arranged in rows, with settings split into Layout,
  Panel, Actions and Manage.
- Settings › Edge panels opens panel settings even while panels are off.
- SSH hosts gain an address to use at home.
- Fixed GUI pinch gestures and Japanese menu text.
