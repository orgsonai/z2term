0.8.415-alpha

- Keyboard face settings now provide enable, reorder, duplicate and edit actions
  together, plus a field for testing keyboard input.
- The layout editor now uses a fixed keyboard preview: tap a key to edit it and
  adjust widths in exact 0.1 steps.
- The symbols (?#) face can now be edited as a child of a letter layout.
- Simple letters are now a built-in face that starts disabled; the global style
  selector has been removed.
- Added restore-to-duplicated-defaults, direct word registration and new user
  dictionary creation.
- Renamed the Tips section to “Tips and tricks.”
