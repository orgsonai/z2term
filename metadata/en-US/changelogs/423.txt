0.8.415-alpha

- Keyboard faces can be enabled, reordered, duplicated and edited in one
  place, with a test input field.
- The layout editor was rebuilt: tap a key on the fixed preview to edit it;
  widths now step by 0.1.
- The symbols (?#) face is editable under a letter layout.
- Simple letters is a new built-in face, off by default; the global style
  selector is gone.
- Added restore to duplicated state, word registration and new dictionaries.
- Tips is now "Tips and tricks."
