0.8.447-alpha

- Added FTP, SMB, WebDAV and VNC services under SSH destinations with SSH port forwarding enabled by default. Blank local ports are selected automatically.
- Improved SMB2/3 and anonymous-share compatibility.
- Android Back now moves to the parent folder and asks before disconnecting only at the root.
- Made the connection list more compact with horizontal actions.
- Added show/hide buttons to password fields in connection settings.
