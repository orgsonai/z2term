0.8.414-alpha

- The on-screen keyboard is yours to shape: edit the key layout on every face
  (kana, letters, symbols), save layouts by name, switch between them. Face and
  layout settings now live in one place.
- Flick popups look the same on every face, and the hint characters no longer
  vanish while your finger is down.
- Paste moved to the top row, and the paste pad closes once you paste.
- Settings gained "Tips": gestures that are not visible on screen.
